Who We Are

Our Story

25+ Years of Experience

Where Tradition
Meets Excellence

Founded on the values of tradition, quality, and hospitality, the journey of Arora Sweets began in 1980 with a simple vision — to serve authentic taste with honesty and care.

Over the years, our family earned the trust of generations through dedication, hard work, and a passion for quality food. In 2011, four brothers came together with a modern vision and started a new chapter by establishing their own workshop, focusing on purity, consistency, and customer satisfaction.

The journey continued with K Lal Restaurant, known for its delicious food and warm hospitality. In 2018, we proudly launched Arora Sweets, blending traditional flavors with modern standards of hygiene and service. With the love and support of our customers, the brand quickly became a trusted name among families and food lovers.

Today, Arora Sweets also proudly serves outdoor catering for weddings, festivals, parties, and special occasions, carrying forward the same promise of quality, taste, and hospitality.

What We Stand For

Our Values

Timeless Recipes

Our recipes are inherited, not invented. Some are over a century old, passed down with reverence and unwavering precision.

Made with Love

Every piece is handcrafted in small batches by our master halwais. No automation, no mass production — only care and craft.

Uncompromised Quality

We source only the best — Kashmiri saffron, Rajasthani ghee, Bengal's finest chenna. Quality is non-negotiable at Arora.

Rooted in Tradition

We celebrate India's festive heritage with seasonal specialties, regional recipes, and culturally authentic creations.

Community First

Our halwais are family. We invest in their growth, their welfare, and their mastery — because Arora is only as good as the people who make it.

A Home for All

Whether it's a festive celebration, a casual craving, or a grand occasion — Arora Sweets is where every moment finds its sweetness.

More than just a sweets shop, Arora Sweets is a symbol of trust, tradition, and family values.
