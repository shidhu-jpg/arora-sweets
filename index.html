Who We Are

A Legacy of
Sweet Excellence

For over 15 years, Arora Sweets has been the destination for those who refuse to compromise on quality. Every mithai is handcrafted in small batches using heirloom recipes, premium ingredients, and a reverence for tradition that no machine can replicate.

Our three outlets across the city have become landmarks — places where memories are made over a box of perfectly crafted sweets.
